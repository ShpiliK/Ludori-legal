# Privacy Policy — Ludori: Game Finder

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Ludori: Game Finder (“Ludori”, “the app”, “we”) is an independent mobile app that helps users discover curated mobile games.

Contact: ludori.app@gmail.com

## Summary

Ludori does not require an account, does not display advertising, does not use analytics, does not process payments, and does not collect or sell personal data.

## Information stored on your device

To make the app work smoothly across launches, Ludori stores a small amount of local app state on your device:

- your selected interface language;
- whether you have acknowledged the legal screen;
- whether you have completed the in-app tutorial.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or to a server. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

## Remote images and technical requests

Ludori loads game cover images from remote storage. Like any standard web request, the hosting provider may process basic technical request information, such as IP address, timestamp, user agent, and the requested file URL, for content delivery, reliability, and security.

We do not use this information to identify users, track users, or build user profiles.

## External links

Ludori contains links to external services such as Google Play, the Apple App Store, and YouTube. When you tap one of these links, it opens in your browser or the relevant store/app. Those services are operated by third parties and may have their own privacy policies.

## Contact features

The “Suggest a game” and “Report a bug” features open your device’s default email client. Ludori does not send these messages automatically. You decide whether to send the email.

If you contact us by email, we will use your message and email address only to respond to your suggestion, question, or bug report.

## No advertising or tracking

Ludori does not show ads, does not use the Android Advertising ID, and does not track users across apps or websites.

## Children

Ludori is not directed specifically to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

## Data deletion

Because Ludori does not create user accounts or collect personal data through the app, there is no account data to delete.

You can remove local app data by uninstalling the app. If you previously contacted us by email and want that email deleted, contact us at ludori.app@gmail.com.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above.
